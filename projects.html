Featured Projects

Our Work

Spaces We've
Transformed

From intimate studios to sprawling penthouses, each project is a unique collaboration between our vision and our clients' lives.

Luxury New York

The Manhattan Penthouse

A 4,200 sq ft full-floor penthouse in Midtown Manhattan. The brief: create a home that felt like a private members' club. Gold-veined marble, bespoke cabinetry, and custom lighting tell the story of quiet luxury.

Minimalist Tokyo

Tokyo Minimalist Studio

A 48m² studio designed to feel like a meditative retreat. Every millimeter planned. Custom joinery hides an entire wardrobe, a full kitchen, and a Murphy bed.

Scandinavian Stockholm

Scandinavian Family Home

A 1960s Swedish villa transformed into a warm family sanctuary. Light oak floors, linen upholstery, and carefully selected antiques create layers of comfort and character.

Modern London

East London Modern Apartment

A three-bedroom conversion in Shoreditch. The challenge: retain the building's industrial character while creating a warm, contemporary family home. Polished concrete meets natural walnut.

Industrial Brooklyn

Brooklyn Warehouse Loft

A former textile factory converted into 6,000 sq ft of dramatic living space. Original brickwork and steel beams preserved and celebrated alongside bespoke furnishings.

Bohemian Marrakech

Marrakech Riad Renovation

A 400-year-old riad lovingly restored and reimagined. Zellij tile, hand-carved plaster, and global textiles collected by the owners over decades come together in a celebration of craft.

Modern Singapore

Singapore Smart Home

A fully integrated smart home where technology disappears into the design. Voice-controlled lighting, hidden screens, and automated climate systems operate behind seamless, beautiful surfaces.

Luxury Dubai

Dubai Villa Master Suite

A 1,200 sq ft master suite designed for a hospitality client. The brief: five-star hotel, private home. Hand-stitched headboard, Italian marble bathroom, and a custom dressing room.
