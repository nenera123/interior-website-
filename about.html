Our Studio

About Us

Our Story

We Believe Spaces Shape Lives

Aesthetic Interiors was founded in 2014 by a small team of designers united by a single belief: that where you live profoundly shapes how you live. Good design isn't decoration — it's a fundamental quality of life.

Over a decade, we've grown from a two-person studio into a team of fifteen designers, architects, and project managers. Our work spans five continents and has been recognized by the world's leading design publications.

But what we're most proud of isn't the awards or the press. It's the clients who tell us that our work changed how they relate to their homes — and to themselves.

Work With Us
Our Approach

Design Philosophy

Three principles guide every project we undertake, from a single room redesign to a complete architectural renovation.

01

Listen First

Every project begins not with our ideas, but with our clients' lives. How do they move through their day? What do they collect? What do they dream of? Design that doesn't begin with listening produces spaces that look good but don't feel right.

02

Material Honesty

We use materials for what they are, not what they pretend to be. Marble should feel like marble. Wood should feel like wood. The tactile experience of genuine materials is irreplaceable, and it's the foundation of spaces that age beautifully.

03

Timeless Over Trendy

Trends are interesting. Timelessness is rare and precious. We design for the way our clients will feel about their homes in twenty years, not for next season's magazine spread. Great design doesn't date — it deepens.

The People

Meet Our Designers

Three of the talented individuals who bring our projects to life — each with a distinct perspective and shared passion for exceptional design.

Sophia Laurent

Principal Designer & Co-Founder

Trained at the École Nationale Supérieure des Arts Décoratifs in Paris, Sophia brings a European sensibility and a deep respect for craft to every project. Her work is characterized by its warmth, restraint, and timeless quality.

Marcus Reid

Creative Director & Co-Founder

With a background in architecture from the Architectural Association, Marcus approaches interior design from the outside in — understanding how structure, light, and space define the experience of a room before any furniture is chosen.

Aiko Tanaka

Senior Designer — Residential

Aiko studied in Kyoto before joining us, and her practice is deeply informed by Japanese principles of wabi-sabi — finding beauty in imperfection and transience. Her spaces are among our most serene and considered.

Aesthetic Interiors
Interior Design Studio

Creating beautiful, functional spaces that reflect the unique personality and lifestyle of each of our clients.
